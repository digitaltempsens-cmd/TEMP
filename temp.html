Who we are

Not just a manufacturer.
A thermal engineering partner.

At Tempsens, we believe the right temperature measurement solution cannot be selected from a catalogue. It needs to be engineered around the process — the environment, the material, the thermal profile, the installation constraints, and the process outcome that actually matters.


Founded in Udaipur, India in 1990, Tempsens has grown from a precision thermocouple manufacturer into a full-spectrum thermal engineering company. Today, our solutions cover contact temperature measurement, non-contact pyrometry, thermal imaging, process heating, industrial furnaces, calibration systems, and high-temperature cabling.

Across steel plants, glass lines, pharmaceutical facilities, cement kilns, refineries, and aerospace test rigs, Tempsens instruments are the ones engineers trust when measurement errors are not an option.

Why Tempsens

What makes the engineering difference

Many suppliers offer temperature sensors. Fewer offer thermal engineering. At Tempsens, the approach has always been to understand the process first — then engineer the solution.

01
Application engineering built in
Every Tempsens solution begins with a process review. Our application engineers assess installation environment, material compatibility, response time requirements, and calibration needs before recommending a product.
02
Full product lifecycle support
From sensor selection and installation guidance to calibration verification, repairs, and replacements — Tempsens supports customers across the full instrument lifecycle. Our NABL-accredited lab ensures measurement traceability.
03
Custom engineering capability
Standard sensors fail in non-standard conditions. Tempsens designs and manufactures custom thermocouples, MI assemblies, and thermal systems for high-vibration, high-pressure, high-corrosion applications.
04
In-house manufacturing & quality assurance
Tempsens controls the full manufacturing chain — raw material selection, sensor assembly, insulation, sheathing, and calibration — under ISO 9001:2015-certified processes.
05
Global reach, local knowledge
With customers across 75+ countries and trained distribution partners, Tempsens provides global product availability alongside application knowledge that reflects real regional operating conditions.
